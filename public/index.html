METHODOLOGY & SAFETY

Know what the model knows

A transparent process for exploring time-series signals — with limits made explicit.

Chronological validation

Training labels end before the holdout period. A gap prevents overlap between feature construction and evaluation.

Causal features

Rolling frequencies, gaps, and co-occurrence features use completed draws only.

Holdout evaluation

Hit rate, precision, recall, mean hits, and ROI describe historical holdout behavior at the selected K.

Payout assumptions

ROI is a transparent sensitivity calculation based on the stated payout and stake assumptions; it is not a recommendation.

Safety and limitations

This workspace is for analysis and research. Lottery outcomes remain uncertain and the model cannot know a future draw.

Analysis only

No betting, execution, or automated wagering flow is included. All times are shown in ICT (UTC+7).
